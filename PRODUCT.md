## Product Principles

1. Lead with backend depth as the primary technical claim — the site's spine is "backend specialist," not a generic full-stack generalist pitch. Frontend work is proof of range, not the headline.
2. Prioritize real, shipped evidence over academic framing: the backend-focused Bank Sinarmas internship is the headline proof of backend hireability; MOVEHAUS (solo, end-to-end, production) is presented as full-stack range, not backend depth — don't overclaim it.
3. Content must read as current: reflect active thesis + internship status rather than a static "CS student" bio that will age immediately.
4. Visual identity is open for full reinvention — prior design choices are evidence of what existed, not a constraint to preserve.
5. Every claim must trace to something confirmed here or provided by the user; no invented metrics, testimonials, or employers.
